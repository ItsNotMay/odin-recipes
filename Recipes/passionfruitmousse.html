Passionfruit mousse

Hello everyone! Today I'll teach you a simple passionfruit mousse recipe to do in these hot summer days.

this recipe is a delightful dessert and so easy to make! Only taking a few ingredients and using only a mixer. it is smooth, tropical and refreshing, the perfect combination!

disclaimer: this recipe is made using a "parts" measuring, meaning you can adjust for whatever the amount you would like; for exampple: if you'd like 600g of mousse you will use 200g of double cream, 200g of condensed milk and 200ml of passionfruit pulp.

Ingredients

  • 1 part of condensed milk
  • 1 part of double cream
  • 1 part of passionfruit pulp

Steps

Time required: 10 minutes

  1. In a mixer, add all the ingredients and mix it for 5 minutes.
  2. pour it in a bowl and put it in the fridge.
  3. let it chill for about 3 to 4 hours.

And this is the recipe! Super easy to make and even easier to enjoy. Now this recipe will make a more sour mousse, if you want it to be sweeter, you can add half a part of passionfruit pulp and slowly adding more until it gets to your liking.

If you also dont want to have seed bits in the mousse, you can use a sieve and a spoon to extract all the pulp from the seeds.
